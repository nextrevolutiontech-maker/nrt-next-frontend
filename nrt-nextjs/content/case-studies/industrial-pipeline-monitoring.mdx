# Industrial Pipeline Monitoring & IoT Telemetry Dashboard

## Executive Summary

Factory floor managers lacked real-time visibility into industrial engineering pipelines, leading to reactive maintenance, unexpected machine downtime, and costly workflow delays.

Next Revolution Tech developed Sistima—a high-throughput IoT monitoring dashboard that streams live sensor data, detects pressure fluctuations using AI threshold rules, and alerts engineering teams instantly.

---

## Solution Highlights

- **WebSocket Live Telemetry**: Renders live pressure, flow rate, and temperature gauges at 60 FPS.
- **Predictive Anomaly Engine**: Machine learning model that predicts valve wear before critical failure occurs.
- **Role-Based Command Center**: Granular control access for maintenance teams, shift supervisors, and plant directors.

---

## Impact

- Unplanned downtime reduced by **50%**.
- Maintenance response times improved from hours to under 3 minutes.
