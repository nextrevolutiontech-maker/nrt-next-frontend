# Financial Compliance & Auditing Platform

## Executive Summary

Manual ledger auditing and receipt logging created compliance risks and slowed down financial reporting cycles for corporate finance managers.

NRT built a secure FinTech auditing platform featuring double-entry compliance checks, tax withholding engines, and automated invoice OCR.

---

## Features

- **Automated Receipt Parsing**: Extracts vendor, tax ID, and line-item amounts instantly.
- **Audit-Trail Logging**: Immutable transaction logs for complete regulatory auditability.
- **Executive Ledger Summary**: Instant profit-and-loss and tax balance reporting.
