# Enterprise HR & Payroll Platform (TrueHRIS)

## Executive Summary

An enterprise conglomerate with 2,500+ employees across 5 regional branches experienced severe delays and errors due to manual attendance logging and disconnected payroll calculation spreadsheets.

NRT engineered TrueHRIS—a unified HR, biometric attendance, tax ledger, and automated payroll platform designed to handle complex salary structures, shift rosters, and tax withholdings.

---

## Key Features Built

1. **Biometric Hardware Integration**: Direct IP camera and fingerprint device webhooks syncing clock-in data in real time.
2. **Automated Tax & Provident Fund Ledger**: Auto-calculated income tax tiers, loan deductions, and bonus disbursements.
3. **Employee Self-Service Portal**: Mobile-responsive portal for leave applications, payslip downloads, and tax certificates.

---

## Business Outcome

- Payroll calculation speed increased by **95%**.
- HR administrative costs dropped by **35%**.
- 100% audit-ready tax reporting compliance.
