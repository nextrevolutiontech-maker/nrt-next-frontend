# Aviation Compliance & Manufacturing Engineering Portal

## Executive Summary

Aviation component manufacturers struggled to track ISO safety certificates and technical CAD drawings through manual filing systems.

NRT created an enterprise directory featuring technical drawing displays, safety certificate registries, and automated RFQ workflows.

---

## Capabilities

- **Secure Document Vault**: Watermarked Technical drawing viewing with role-based permissions.
- **Safety Expiry Alerts**: Automated notifications 60 days prior to ISO certification renewals.
- **Supplier RFQ Portal**: Fast tender dispatching for specialized component machining.
