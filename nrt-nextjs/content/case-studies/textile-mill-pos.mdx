# Textile Mill Yarn Batch ERP & Multi-Branch POS Platform

## Executive Summary

A premier textile manufacturing group operating 3 weaving plants and 8 retail factory outlets required an end-to-end ERP and POS solution capable of tracking raw cotton yarn batches through spinning, dyeing, weaving, and final retail point-of-sale.

Next Revolution Tech developed a custom industrial ERP and POS system featuring offline-first retail billing terminals, automated FBR (Federal Board of Revenue) tax webhooks, and raw material batch tracking.

---

## The Challenge

1. **Yarn Batch Tracking Errors**: Difficulty tracing fabric defects back to specific yarn dyeing lots resulted in costly customer returns.
2. **FBR Tax Compliance Delays**: Manual entry of wholesale invoices into the tax portal delayed daily logistics dispatches.
3. **Retail POS Stock Outages**: Factory outlets struggled with delayed inventory sync during high-volume sale events.

---

## The NRT Solution

- **Yarn Lot & Loom Telemetry**: Serialized QR code tagging for every fabric roll and yarn batch.
- **Automated FBR Webhooks**: Direct API integration issuing real-time digital tax invoices at the exact moment of sale clearance.
- **Offline-First POS Terminals**: High-speed retail billing software that processes transactions offline during internet outages and syncs automatically when reconnected.

---

## Results & Impact

- **Automated Tax Filing**: 100% compliance with automated FBR QR code invoice printing on every receipt.
- **Traceability**: Reduced fabric returns by 65% through instant lot diagnostics.
- **Uninterrupted Sales**: Zero downtime across retail outlets during seasonal rush sales.
