# Global Supply Chain Coordination System (Austrades)

## Executive Summary

B2B material suppliers struggled with disconnected logistics tracking and manual order dispatching, causing severe operational bottlenecks across international shipping routes.

NRT designed and deployed Austrades—a custom business software hub connecting suppliers, freight forwarders, and port customs in real time.

---

## Technical Architecture

- **Multi-Carrier API Aggregation**: Integrates with DHL, Maersk, and local logistics webhooks.
- **Customs Document OCR**: Automatically parses bill of lading documents and validates HS tariff codes.
- **Client Tracking Portal**: Live shipment visibility for enterprise buyers with automated status notifications.
