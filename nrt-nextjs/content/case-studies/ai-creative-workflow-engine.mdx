# AI-Powered Creative Workflow Engine (NeuraHub)

## Executive Summary

Marketing teams faced severe bottlenecks due to manual content creation, fragmented AI tools, and uncoordinated social media scheduling.

NRT engineered NeuraHub—an automated AI aggregator that orchestrates multi-modal AI models (LLMs, image generators, video synthesis) into a cohesive enterprise workspace.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Agentic Workflow Chains**: Automates prompt execution from raw brief to multi-channel graphics.
- **Brand Style Enforcer**: Guardrail engine ensuring all generated copy matches brand tone and guidelines.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Tracks engagement metrics and auto-tunes future prompt templates.
